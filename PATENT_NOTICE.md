## Patent

execution governance are the subject of US Patent Application No. 19/575,615,
"Methods and Systems for Structured Compatibility Certification and Execution
Control in One or More External Systems" (patent pending).

## Scope

The patented RSCT technology is implemented in:
- RSCT certification methodology (covered in swarm-it-api)
- Quality gate systems for AI execution (covered in swarm-it-api)

This repository (swarm-it-auth) provides **authentication and session management** infrastructure that supports the Swarm-It platform but does not itself implement the patented RSCT technology.
